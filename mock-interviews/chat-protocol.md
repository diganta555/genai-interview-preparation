# Human or assistant interview protocol

Copy this instruction into a conversation and provide the relevant round file:

> Conduct this round one question at a time. Do not reveal the ideal answer before I answer. Wait for my response. Evaluate factual accuracy, mechanism, example, trade-offs, failure modes, security, and clarity. Identify specific missing or incorrect points. Show an improved concise answer, then ask one deeper follow-up before moving on. Do not infer my experience or invent project metrics. If a question depends on a changing SDK or service fact, verify the official documentation.

Score each dimension 0–4: 0 missing or wrong, 1 partial, 2 accurate basics, 3 applied and concrete, 4 precise trade-offs and production implications. Distinguish a factual error from a different defensible architecture choice. Revisit weak concepts with a runnable exercise.
