# Official sources and maintenance

The chapters contain original teaching explanations; these primary references support checking APIs and mechanisms. Links were checked during authoring on 2026-10-03. Package/model availability and service limits must be verified in your own deployment environment.

- [LangChain overview](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langchain/overview) — modern agent and provider interface references.
- [LangGraph persistence](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/persistence) — checkpoint concepts.
- [LangGraph interrupts](https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/interrupts) — pause/resume and replay boundaries.
- [FastAPI async](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/async/) and [containers](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/deployment/docker/) — concurrency and deployment.
- [Pydantic models](https://pydantic.dev/docs/validation/latest/concepts/models/) — runtime validation.
- [Chroma updates](https://docs.trychroma.com/docs/collections/update-data) — update and upsert behavior.
- [LlamaIndex starter](https://developers.llamaindex.ai/python/framework/getting_started/starter_example/) — document indexing patterns.
- [Bedrock identity and access](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/security-iam.html) — IAM and temporary credential concepts.
- [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) — original transformer research.
- [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) and [QLoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314) — adaptation research.
- [Python documentation](https://docs.python.org/3/), [FAISS](https://github.com/facebookresearch/faiss), [Qdrant](https://qdrant.tech/documentation/), [Weaviate](https://docs.weaviate.io/), and [Pinecone](https://docs.pinecone.io/) — further official reference links; individual features were not all independently tested.

Do not memorize volatile prices or model names from a study guide. Store a tested configuration and update it through evaluation and review.
